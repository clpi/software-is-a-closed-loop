## How axiom lists are reported here

Per module, the census lists the axiom dependencies printed in the checker log for the theorems that log prints. "Empty" means the log line reads "does not depend on any axioms". "Standard" means some subset of the three standard foundational axioms named above. Theorems present in a file but not printed in the checker log are noted as not printed, where that matters. For the new modules, the readme's own statements and the log's prints are the evidence; where the log does not print a new module's theorems individually, the entry says so.

### 26. GeneralPareto — the general Pareto obligations, discharged (17 declarations)

- Obligation 1, bounds-derivation soundness: the derived interval for an assignment and axis is the minimum and maximum of the per-workload aggregated values; the soundness theorem proves every collected value lies inside it; the attainment theorem proves each end of a nonempty interval is the aggregated value of a named workload (the discharge condition); an extension theorem bounds individual executions above under the worst-case aggregation.
- Obligation 2, aggregation well-definedness: the maximum, minimum, and sum aggregations are each independent of the execution list's order (three permutation theorems); the maximum and minimum characterizations prove each is a total function into the optional type, returning no value exactly on the empty list — the unwitnessed cell.
Also: the three-outcome decision on bounds is proved — separation of the candidate's interval below the incumbent's on every axis, strict on one, implies every realized candidate vector strictly Pareto-dominates every realized incumbent vector (two theorems); overlap on any axis proves neither direction separated (the undecided case); a minimal-element existence lemma; strict Pareto irreflexivity and transitivity in this layer; and frontier completeness — every member of a named candidate list is in its strict-Pareto frontier or is strictly dominated by a frontier member.
