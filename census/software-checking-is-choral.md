## The thesis, in plain English

A cold gate that splits by derivation cone, with each
cone checked by a different checker and the verdicts
joined, accepts exactly what the whole check accepts.
The cones exchange their checked artifacts along every
cross-cone dependency, and the joined axiom audit is
the whole gate audit.

## What is proved

Fourteen theorem declarations in two Lean files, at
the pinned toolchain named in the tree (a Lean release
in the estate usual family pin line), core only:

- The cone point result (one theorem, with its fuel and
  fold lemmas): a cone values equal the whole check
  values on every module of the cone, when the cone
  boundary agrees with the whole check outside the cone.
- The joined verdict result: the joined verdict over a
  covering family of three cones is the whole verdict,
  in both directions. Coverage is the only demand on
  the family; no disjointness is demanded.
- The audit point and audit join results: a module
  audit agrees between the cone environment and the
  whole environment, and the audit list of a joined
  module list is the join of the cone audit lists.
- The supporting lemmas: fuel above rank never changes
  a value, in both the whole and the cone evaluation,
  and the whole value unfolds over its dependencies.

Axiom state, as the repo readme and its audit file
record at the pin: the two fuel and fold results depend

depend on the two standard principles named in the
audit file (propositional extensionality and quotient
soundness) only; the two controls depend on
propositional extensionality only. Nothing depends on

## Premises, named and not proved here

- The rank premise: the dependency graph is acyclic,
  witnessed by a rank function decreasing along every
  dependency. The level assignment of a real import
  graph is such a rank.
- The boundary premise: the values a cone reads outside
  itself are the whole check values there — the join
  contract that exchanged artifacts are the producing
  cone own checked outputs, carried unchanged.
- The audit premise: a module audit reads the checking
  environment only at the module and its dependencies.
- Each checker runs the toolchain the subject
  development pins. This is an operational premise of
  the experiment, not a premise of the theorems.
